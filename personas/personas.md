## Infrastructure Admin

Source: [admin-user.png](admin-user.png)

**David Smith** — *“Infrastructure Admin”*

- 42
- California
- Married

### Bio

I manage the whole domain and make technical propositions to my managers.

I am qualified and have several years of experience. There is a good chance I started on a helpdesk and understand call centres and tech support structures.

### Needs and expectations

- Our environments are mostly hybrid environment, in large companies. Transitioning from On Prem.
- Able to administer group policies, automation tasks, and workflows,

### Motivations

- Object management and automation of tasks that can be delegated whilst I setup more complex policies and workflows
- Easier ways to automate hybrid environment tasks, and provision helpdesk users with ARS.\
  Better ways to delegate permissions with active roles.\
  Wants RestAPI

### Pain points and frustrations

- I don’t have time for WebUI to load, or process actions that take seconds in MMC.\
  Does not want SaaS to manage B2B B2C.

## NON-Technical User for UserView

Source: [self-service-end-user.png](self-service-end-user.png)

**Lisa Fernandez** — *“People Operations Manager”*

- 38
- Chicago, IL
- Married

### Bio

I oversee our employee experience from onboarding to offboarding. My focus is making sure our people feel supported, our policies are clear, and our tools actually help rather than slow us down. I rely on my team leads for input and work closely with department heads on hiring and retention.

### Needs and expectations

- An easy-to-use HRIS that doesn't require IT support for everyday tasks.
- Self-service portals for employees to update their own info and access pay stubs.

### Motivations

- I want to create a workplace where people genuinely enjoy coming to work and feel valued.
- Streamlining our hiring pipeline so we can fill roles faster without sacrificing quality.

### Pain points and frustrations

- Our current tools are clunky and outdated. I spend more time on data entry and workarounds than on actual people strategy. Reporting is a nightmare — I can never pull the data I need without asking IT for help.
